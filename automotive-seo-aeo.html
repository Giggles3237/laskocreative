One connected strategy

SEO builds the foundation.
AEO sharpens the answer.

Not a replacement for SEO. Not a shortcut to guaranteed AI recommendations. Two complementary ways to make your dealership easier to understand and discover.

Search engine optimization

Make important pages accessible, relevant and useful for search engines and people. Address technical obstacles, local intent, page structure, internal links and content quality.

Answer engine optimization

Understand the questions people ask AI assistants and answer-led search experiences. Build clear, specific, well-supported answers that explain your dealership, its services and its differences.

From a gap to a useful page

Start with what customers need to know.

A shopper comparing models needs a different answer from an owner looking for service. A dealership website should help both find the right next step, without asking them to sort through vague marketing copy.

The work starts with your current visibility, the questions that matter to your market and the pages already on your site. Then we identify gaps worth addressing, rather than publishing more content for its own sake.

What an engagement can include

  • Visibility and content review: understand how your dealership appears in selected search and AI-answer experiences, and where important questions are unanswered.
  • Prioritized page plan: choose meaningful opportunities across vehicle research, service, ownership and local dealership selection.
  • Useful page content: direct answers, clear explanations, accurate details, internal links and an appropriate next step.
  • Technical and publishing coordination: work within your website platform, review crawlability and metadata, and recommend structured data only where it accurately reflects the page.
  • Measurement and iteration: review available search, web and AI-visibility signals alongside the changes made, then decide what to improve next.

Scope is agreed around your dealership’s needs, available access and capacity to publish. Content and factual claims are reviewed before release.

Dealership context, not generic content volume.

Sales, service, parts and ownership questions have different jobs to do. BMW and MINI, for example, need their own brand language and answers, not one interchangeable set of pages. The same principle applies to every dealership: start with what is genuinely true and useful for that business.

Reporting that explains the work.

A useful report should show the period measured, the signals available, the actions completed and the next priorities. AI-answer visibility can vary by platform, prompt and timing; it is a sampled signal, not a count of every shopper. Website traffic and leads provide different evidence. Correlation is not proof that a particular page caused a sale.

A few straight answers

Before we start.

Does AEO replace SEO?

No. Search engines and AI-answer experiences still need accessible, useful information. Technical SEO, relevant content and clear business information remain important foundations. AEO adds attention to how questions are answered and how a business is represented in AI-generated responses.

Can you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. Search rankings, inclusion and AI citations are controlled by outside platforms. The commitment is to useful work, factual content, transparent measurement and a clear improvement plan, not a guaranteed placement.

Will this work with our current website?

The plan should fit your existing platform and publishing process. Access, templates and technical limits are reviewed first. Content can be prepared for your team or website provider to review and deploy; changes are not assumed to happen automatically.

What would you need from us?

A conversation about priorities, your website and brand guidance, accurate dealership information, and the relevant reporting or access you can authorize. We can start with a scoped review rather than a large technology project.
