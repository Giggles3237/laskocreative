QRPhoto

A shared photo collection, one scan away.

Web app

The idea

QRPhoto is an event-photo sharing web app. A QR-led experience connects guests with a shared collection, making it easier to gather different perspectives on the same occasion.

What it is designed to help with

  • A practical use for a printed QR code
  • Shared event-photo collections
  • A creative product built around people and their memories

Where it stands

Web app. You can visit the product website to explore its current offering.
