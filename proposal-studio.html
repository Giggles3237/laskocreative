Proposal Studio

A more personal way to present the next vehicle.

Browser-based tool

The idea

Proposal Studio brings quote information, brochure details and an advisor’s personal note into branded HTML. The focus is presenting the supplied information clearly, not recalculating a deal.

What it is designed to help with

  • BMW and MINI presentation styles
  • Optional personal note and advisor details
  • Customer-ready HTML from the supplied materials

Where it stands
