The idea

Creative Companion explores a touch-first studio with guided paths for songs, stories, cards, artwork and printables. Instead of starting with a blank prompt, a person starts with what they want to make.

What it is designed to help with

  • Guided choices instead of a blank page
  • Songs, stories, cards and creative projects
  • A prototype that makes room for experimentation
